# Psalm 23: Hand-Painted Video Workflow

## Goal
Create a 60-second, 16:9 Psalm 23 video in which a visible hand paints an oil landscape, accompanied by the existing Psalm 23 narration.

## References
- Approved Artistly painting: Psalm_23_Artistly_Painting.jpg (ChatGPT Library: libfile_78f62b784e408191b095076098f07a54).
- Approved starting image: Hand painting a golden sunset.png (ChatGPT Library: libfile_8410e36bb18881918789f6338504c853).
- Video Express project: Psalm 23 Hand Painting - 60s Work in Progress.
- Video Express review export: Psalm 23 Hand Painting - 60s Review.

## Production method
1. Create a textured oil painting reference in Artistly in landscape format.
2. Prepare a starting image showing a natural hand holding a brush against a partly painted canvas.
3. Import the starting image into Video Express. Use Create with AI > Create Video From Prompt, landscape 16:9.
4. Direct one continuous, fixed shot: the hand holds the brush naturally, bristles contact the canvas, and oil paint appears where they move. Preserve the existing painting, canvas texture, lighting and framing. Exclude extra hands, camera moves, speech and music.
5. In Advanced Mode choose Video Only and Manual Video Length; set the desired duration (up to 10 seconds in the observed controls).
6. After generation, play the clip and check hand anatomy, brush contact, paint appearance, continuity and camera stability.
7. In My AI Videos, use the clip's context menu > Save Last Frame. Name it Psalm23_C##_End and use it as the next clip's starting image. Generate, inspect and repeat.
8. Add the clips sequentially to the timeline. Put the existing Psalm 23 voiceover on a separate track. Save the project.
9. Export a Full HD MP4 review and check the entire video, duration, joins, narration and ending before approval.
